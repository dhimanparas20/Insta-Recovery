First: try to get the original account back

A recovered account keeps all its followers, posts, DMs and highlights; rebuilding can't bring all of that back. Appeal first, and keep going with this hub while you wait:

  1. Appeal in the app. Log in to the disabled account: the notice offers a way to disagree with the decision and says how long you have. Don't miss that window. Save every reference number and reply.
  2. Hacked? Use instagram.com/hacked with the email or phone number on the account.
  3. If the appeal fails, check whether your country has another route. India: Meta's Grievance Officer, then the Grievance Appellate Committee within 30 days of their reply. EU: a certified out-of-court dispute settlement body under the Digital Services Act.

Meta's Terms §3.1: if they disable your account, "you agree not to create another account without our permission". A new account can be removed for this, so move slowly: no bursts of follows or posts.
