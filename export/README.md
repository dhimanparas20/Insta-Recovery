# Put your Instagram export here

Drop the zip file(s) from Instagram's "Download your information" into this folder, then double-click `START_HERE.bat` (Windows) or run `python3 recover.py build` (Mac/Linux).

- Keep the zips as they are; the kit unzips them into `unzipped/` by itself. If the download came in several parts, put all of them here.
- An already-unzipped folder works too.
- Everything in this folder is your personal data. It stays on your computer and is ignored by git. Don't upload it anywhere.
